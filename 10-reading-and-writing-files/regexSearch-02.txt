Making bread is mostly a matter of waiting. You mix flour, water, salt and
a little yeast, and then you leave the dough alone while it does the
interesting part without you.

The first loaf I ever baked came out dense enough to prop open a door. My
mistake was impatience: I punched the dough down after twenty minutes
because nothing seemed to be happening. Something was happening, just not
fast enough to see.

Now I set a timer for four hours and go do something else entirely. The
bread is better, and so is the afternoon.
