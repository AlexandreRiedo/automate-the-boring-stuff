Trains in this country run on a timetable that has not changed since 1974,
which means the schedule is either a triumph of engineering or a very
patient joke.

The 6:12 to the coast is always half empty. There is a man who sits in the
fourth carriage with a folding chessboard, playing both sides against
himself and losing to whichever colour he happens to prefer that week.

I have taken that train eleven times and never once seen him finish a
game. He packs up two stops early, every time, and steps off without
looking back at the board.
